# The Finding
### To the Dashboard Team,

The dashboard reported that certain accounts were associated with disputed trips, leading to driver bonuses being frozen.
However, the data distinguishes between riders who request trips and drivers who complete them. 
A rider appearing in a disputed trip does not, by itself, establish that the driver should lose a bonus. 
The trip records identify both parties, so the correct driver must be determined from the driver information attached to each disputed trip.

The error came from focusing on the riders involved in disputed trips rather than correctly identifying the drivers associated with those trips. 
This can lead to the wrong people being flagged when deciding whose bonuses to freeze.
The dashboard's results should be reviewed against the actual trip records, using the driver associated with each disputed trip rather than the rider's account.

Please keep the affected bonuses on hold only until the driver records and applicable bonus rules have been verified.
Any bonus frozen solely because of this reporting error should be released, 
while any case with a separately verified reason to withhold payment should remain under review. 
The dashboard should also be corrected and checked before it is used for future bonus decisions.
