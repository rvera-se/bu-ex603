# The Memo
**To: Platform Leadership**

**Subject: Recommended actions for drivers with repeated cancellations and drivers with no recorded trips**

The trip records show two different situations that require different responses.

**Drivers with repeated cancellations:** Drivers 11, 2, 6, and 9 have the highest numbers of cancelled trips: 5, 4, 4, and 4, respectively.
Leadership should review their cancellation patterns and the recorded reasons, then contact drivers whose records show repeated availability problems.
They may benefit from schedule adjustments, availability updates, or support resolving operational issues. However, a cancelled trip does not automatically
mean the driver was responsible; some cancellations were attributed to rider no-shows, rider cancellations, or payment failures.

**Drivers who never started:** Drivers 14 and 15 have no recorded trips.
Their situation is different because there is no trip history to evaluate.
Leadership should first verify whether they completed onboarding, activated their accounts, and are available to accept trips.
If they are expected to be active, leadership should contact them to understand what prevented their first trip and help them get started.

The key distinction is that drivers with repeated cancellations need a review of existing activity, 
while drivers with no trips need onboarding and activation follow-up.
The records identify these patterns, but leadership must investigate before concluding why they occurred.
